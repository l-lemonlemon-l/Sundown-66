DRIVE
Arrows or WASD. Space or Enter to start. B or Shift uses a boost. You carry three, and they refill every third town. C buys gas ($6 for a fifth of a tank). R changes the radio. F for full screen.
SHOOT
Aim with the mouse, press E or click to fire at a cop's rear tires. Q swaps between your main gun and sidearm. On touch, FIRE aims for you and tapping the gun panel swaps.
TOUCH
Hold to gas, let go to brake, tilt to steer. Double-tap to boost, swipe down to buy gas, two-finger tap for the radio. The crosshair aims and fires by itself.
RACE FRIENDS
Pick Race Friends after the title. Host a race to get a 4-letter room code, or join with a friend's code. The host picks the state: Illinois, Missouri, Kansas, Oklahoma, Texas, New Mexico, Arizona or California. Top finishers win cash.
GARAGE AND ARMORY
Shopping happens before a run. P opens car parts in the garage. In the armory pick a main gun and a sidearm and press M for gun mods. Your cash and everything you buy carry over between runs.
25¢ PLAY

People jaywalk across Route 66. They dive out of the way, but every near miss raises your HEAT, and enough heat brings the cops. Three cop bumps and you are busted. You earn cash every second you stay ahead of the Baron, and spinning out a cop pays a bounty. Every town gives you a speed boost. Grab red fuel cans and keep your headlights on the road after sundown.
